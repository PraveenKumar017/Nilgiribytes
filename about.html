Our story

Warm Greetings from Kotagiri

Nilgiribytes, Kotagiri — manufacturers of authentic Ooty Varkey since 1980, with 45+ years of baking tradition behind every batch.

Retail & wholesale varkey and biscuits, made with a commitment to quality. Pre-order cakes also available. Every batch is still made the traditional way, in small quantities through the day, so nothing sits waiting on a shelf.
